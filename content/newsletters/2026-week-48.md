This issue is mostly about things that change quietly: bills that grow without one big line item, defaults that shift in a major upgrade, and web server configs that suddenly need a security check. Our view is that a cost nobody checks is an incident on a slow timer, and it deserves the same measure-first treatment you would give an outage. Nearly every post here starts by measuring or taking stock of what you already run before you change anything, and we think that is the right order.
