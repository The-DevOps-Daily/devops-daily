This issue is mostly about guardrails and what they actually stop, from an AI agent working behind a read_only Postgres role to a local AWS emulator that let a user with no policies assume an admin role. Our take: a permission you have not tried to break is a guess, so send the request that should fail before you trust a role, an emulator or a kill switch. The Neon test makes the same point from the other side, since every write was blocked and the real risk turned out to be a read that stalled a migration.
