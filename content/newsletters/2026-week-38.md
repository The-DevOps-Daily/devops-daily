## Blog Posts

### [Figma Made Multiplayer Instant by Picking the Dumber Algorithm](https://devops-daily.com/posts/figma-multiplayer-dumber-algorithm)

![Figma Made Multiplayer Instant by Picking the Dumber Algorithm](/images/posts/figma-multiplayer-dumber-algorithm.png)

Figma skipped Operational Transforms and did not build a real CRDT either. What they shipped instead is simpler than both, and it is the reason multiplayer feels instant.

### [systemd and NetworkManager Are Trapping AI Agents](https://devops-daily.com/posts/ai-canaries-systemd-networkmanager)

![systemd and NetworkManager Are Trapping AI Agents](/images/posts/ai-canaries-systemd-networkmanager.png)

systemd and NetworkManager are now planting instructions in their repos so an unreviewed AI contribution gives itself away. Worth a read if you maintain anything that accepts outside pull requests.

### [Gate Your Terraform Plans: Rules Decide, the Model Explains](https://devops-daily.com/posts/terraform-plan-gate-digitalocean-inference)

![Gate Your Terraform Plans: Rules Decide, the Model Explains](/images/posts/terraform-plan-gate-digitalocean-inference.png)

A plan that says "3 to add, 1 to change, 1 to destroy" tells reviewers almost nothing, and they approve it anyway. This walks through a setup where rules make the call and the model only has to explain it.

### [How Netflix Ships a Third of the Internet: The CDN They Had to Build](https://devops-daily.com/posts/how-netflix-ships-a-third-of-the-internet-open-connect)

![How Netflix Ships a Third of the Internet: The CDN They Had to Build](/images/posts/how-netflix-ships-a-third-of-the-internet-open-connect.png)

At its 2015 peak Netflix was 37% of North American downstream traffic, and almost none of it came from a commercial CDN. The story of why they had to build their own is mostly a story about hardware.

### [In-App, Email and Push From One Event](https://devops-daily.com/posts/in-app-email-and-push-from-one-event)

![In-App, Email and Push From One Event](/images/posts/in-app-email-and-push-from-one-event.png)

One order-shipped event, three channels, and a user who wants a badge now, an email later, and no push at all. Here is how to route that without writing the same logic three times.

### [Meta Says Every Muse User Gets Their Own VM](https://devops-daily.com/posts/meta-muse-gives-every-user-a-vm)

![Meta Says Every Muse User Gets Their Own VM](/images/posts/meta-muse-gives-every-user-a-vm.png)

Meta says its new consumer agent gets a dedicated cloud VM per person, with a second agent keeping watch on the first. We look at what that isolation actually buys you and what it costs to run.
